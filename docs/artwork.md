### Street

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic interactive narrative game environment backdrop, landscape 16:9 image. Create an original prestige independent Australian film still: a quiet residential and small shop street in Cabramatta southwest Sydney at night in constant light rain. Camera at human eye level from footpath on left looking down long street toward vanishing point at right center. In the left third foreground a narrow dark brick Vietnamese nail salon behind a corrugated awning, delicately glowing amber windows, closed security screen door, a small readable sign “NAILS”. A solitary realistically proportioned adult woman silhouette in the warm doorway near center left. Old brick terraces and two-story units recede to right, overhead suburban power lines, parked muted cars, wet asphalt reflecting amber window light, small convenience shop far away, sparse trees. Authentic ordinary Sydney suburbs, not futuristic neon city or Asian alley. Cinematography: anamorphic 35mm photography, fine film grain, deep charcoal and night blue shadows, subdued desaturated olive and brick, warm sodium light and warm cream lit interiors. Frame thoughtfully balanced with dark negative space across upper left and upper center for UI, and across bottom for captions. The image must feel beautiful, quietly human and atmospheric; rich details without clutter. No overlays, no UI, no text other than tiny nail shop sign, no watermark. Not pixel art, illustration or a poster. Dark readable cinematic photograph with real brick texture and rain on pavement.
