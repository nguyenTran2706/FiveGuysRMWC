# Official information and contact references

Checked on 12 September 2026. This is a build reference, not legal advice or a clinical review. RMWC should approve public wording and recheck time-sensitive material before launch. The story's dialogue and outcomes are fictional.

| Topic | Source and implementation boundary |
| --- | --- |
| RMWC contacts | [RMWC contact page](https://migrants.org.au/contact/) confirms 1300 513 107, Monday–Friday 9 am–5 pm, hello@migrants.org.au, and Suite 4, Level 3, 377 Sussex Street, Sydney. The app makes no response-time promise. |
| Existing intake | [RMWC homepage](https://migrants.org.au/) links to [Legal Help](https://migrants.org.au/legal-help). The legal-help page could not be retrieved during this check; the brief identifies a Microsoft Forms clinic enquiry. The game links to RMWC's own page instead of inventing a direct form URL or prefill integration. |
| Visa and workplace rights | [Fair Work: Visa holders and migrants](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/visa-holders-migrants) explains workplace rights regardless of migration status and confirms the 13 13 94 Infoline. A visa condition does not make underpayment permissible. |
| Visa protection pilots | [Fair Work: Visa protections—pilot programs](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/visa-holders-migrants/visa-protections-pilot-programs) describes the Workplace Justice Visa and Strengthening Reporting Protections pilots. Eligibility, certification and conditions apply. The app must not promise eligibility or blanket immunity from cancellation. |
| Student working hours | [Fair Work: Visa holders and migrants](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/visa-holders-migrants) includes the 48-hours-per-fortnight student example and explains that visa rules do not remove workplace entitlements. Do not simplify the hours limit to 48 hours per week or state that it applies identically to every student. |
| Pay, contracting, discrimination and harassment | [Fair Work: Migrant worker rights fact sheet](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/fact-sheets/rights-and-obligations/visa-holders-and-migrant-workers-workplace-rights-and-entitlements) links to relevant general rights information. Flags in the game describe possible concerns, not findings of wrongdoing. |
| Dismissal | [Fair Work: Unfair dismissal](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/ending-employment/unfair-dismissal) is a general referral resource. The game does not calculate eligibility or a filing deadline from incomplete information. |
| Bullying | [Fair Work: Bullying in the workplace](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/bullying-in-the-workplace) provides general information. A game comparison alone does not determine a legal category. |
| Crisis support | [Lifeline crisis support](https://www.lifeline.org.au/get-help/national-services/lifeline-crisis-support) confirms 13 11 14. |
| Immediate emergency | [Australian Government: Triple Zero](https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/media-communications/phone/triple-zero) confirms 000 for urgent emergencies. |
| Modern slavery reporting | [Australian Anti-Slavery Commissioner: Report suspected modern slavery](https://www.antislaverycommissioner.gov.au/find-help-and-support/report-suspected-modern-slavery) and [Attorney-General's Department: Support services](https://www.ag.gov.au/crime/modern-slavery/modern-slavery-support-services) confirm AFP 131 237. Present only as an optional reporting contact; do not submit reports on someone's behalf. |

No minimum pay rate, specific immigration eligibility or guaranteed recovery amount is encoded as a legal determination. The RMWC team can advise on the person's actual circumstances.
