# Rain recording credits

Downloaded and source pages checked 17 September 2026. These are recordings of real rain, not synthesised noise or generated audio. They do not claim to depict the story's actual location. Original MP3 bytes are preserved; the application applies loop crossfades, gain automation and a low-pass filter to the storm layer at playback.

| Bundled asset | Original recording | Creator | Source and licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rain-calm.mp3` | `Rain MP3.zip` → `3.mp3` (45 s) | Ylmir | [Rain (loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable), CC0 |
| `rain-heavy.mp3` | `Rain MP3.zip` → `2.mp3` (26 s) | Ylmir | [Rain (loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable), CC0 |
| `rain-thunder.mp3` | `0740.mp3`, Rain and Thunder #2 (about 2:38) | Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank | [Rain and Thunder #2](https://bigsoundbank.com/rain-and-storm-2-s0740.html), CC0 |

Ylmir describes window recordings made with a mono microphone and processed for stereo presentation. The two takes were selected using measured signal levels (quieter take 3, denser/louder take 2); subjective naturalness and mix still require human listening review. BigSoundBank describes an outdoor stereo XY recording of heavy rain and thunder. It includes rain, not an isolated thunder effect.

Source downloads: [Ylmir MP3 archive](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Rain%20MP3.zip), [BigSoundBank MP3](https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/0740.mp3).

CC0 reference: [Creative Commons public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credits are retained for provenance even where attribution is not required. No voice recordings are supplied by these authors.
